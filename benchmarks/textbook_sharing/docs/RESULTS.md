## What sharing gains and costs (main grid)

Source: `results/analysis/cost_decomposition.json`.  1061 pairs (one case, one sharing mode against the unshared layout of the same case) in which both layouts pass every check.  Smaller / larger: area change beyond +-0.5 %.

| mode | pairs | smaller | larger | mean change | min | max | a passive row sets the width |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| diffusion_only | 419 | 341 | 8 | -2.3 % | -9.1 % | +4.6 % | 47 |
| gate_only | 321 | 183 | 2 | -4.4 % | -14.3 % | +0.8 % | 13 |
| combined | 321 | 279 | 11 | -5.5 % | -18.4 % | +18.2 % | 15 |

**Where the 21 area increases come from.**  Each increase A1 - A0 is split exactly into five terms (definitions in `analysis/cost_decomposition.py`); a pair can have several positive terms.

| term | pairs with this term | largest term |
|---|---:|---:|
| extra routing tracks (taller channels) | 16 | 14 |
| width outside the rows to keep the metal-2 stubs of two rows apart (8 by shifting a row, 3 by splitting a net into two trunks joined beyond the row ends) | 11 | 5 |
| wider row that sets the width (shared cell wider) | 2 | 2 |
| taller MOS rows | 2 | 0 |

19 of 21 increases come from routing and row assembly only (8 have both assembly terms).  In the other 2 (C11_sky130_sW1.5_sL1 gate_only, C11_sky130_sW1_sL2 gate_only) the shared cell itself is wider: the gate straps that overlap on the bottom side move to the top, and a top strap's contact pad sits beyond the end of the active area, so the PMOS row grows by 0.88 um while the layout gets 0.58 um lower.

**Interface comparison** (OpenAI model, same tasks, settings and budget).  With an interface that offers only what base gLayout expresses per group, 41 of 42 sessions end at the area of the unshared layout (mean change -0.01 %); with the sharing interface the mean change is -13.1 %.  The comparison measures what an interface lets a model express, not how well a model searches.

Of the 84 Anthropic agent sessions, 44 made only allowed tool calls, 39 also edited their own proposal files with text tools, and 2 were flagged automatically; on manual review one was benign and one read the output file of its own backgrounded submit command (its own tool report); 0 sessions accessed outside information.
